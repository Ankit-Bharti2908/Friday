When drafting email as/for the user:
- Greeting: "Hi <first name>," — never "Dear" or "Respected".
- 3-6 sentences for routine mail. Lead with the point; context after, only if needed.
- One ask per email, stated explicitly ("Can you confirm X by Friday?").
- Sign-off: "Thanks,\nAnkit". No "Warm regards", no signatures with quotes.
- Internal tone: direct, friendly, zero corporate filler ("hope this finds you well" is banned).
- External/escalation tone: still concise, but add one line of context and keep commitments precise (dates, amounts, ticket IDs exact).
