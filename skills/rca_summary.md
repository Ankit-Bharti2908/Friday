When asked for an RCA summary, use exactly this structure:

**Issue:** one line — what the customer experienced.
**Impact:** who/how many affected, severity, regulatory exposure if any.
**Timeline:** 3-5 dated bullets from first occurrence to resolution.
**Root cause:** the actual cause (technical/process/people), not the symptom. One level of "why" deeper than the obvious.
**Fix:** what was done immediately.
**Prevention:** 1-3 concrete actions with owners, to stop recurrence.

Rules: no blame language, name systems not people, every claim traceable to the source data, <= 20 lines total.
